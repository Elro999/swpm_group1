# synthesis/: instructions for agents

## What's here
Team-level analysis built *from* `research/` using the methods in `course_material/`. Files are numbered in the order they were made.

| File | What it is | Feeds |
|---|---|---|
| `01_personas-jtbd-and-prioritisation.md` | Theme matrix across all 25 interviews, the 15 teammate personas merged into 3 team personas + anti-persona, JTBD statements, and the persona prioritisation (including our 8–10 h prototype constraint) | Assignment 2: Personas, JTBD, Problems slides; MVP scoping |

## Rules
- Every claim cites participant codes (e.g. `SUR-1, SAM-4`). Transcripts beat persona files when they disagree.
- Label 1–2-interview patterns as *hypotheses* (S2).
- New analysis goes in a new numbered file; to revise an existing one, edit it and log what changed and why.

## Logging (mandatory)
Append an entry at the bottom of this folder's `log.md` for every change (date, who, **What**, **Why**, **Files**), and in the root `log.md` if you touched other folders too. Never edit past entries.

## Privacy
Participant codes only. No names, employers or universities tied to a participant.
