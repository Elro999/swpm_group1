### Visual system (apply to every slide)
- **Font:** Arial throughout. Slide title 28–32 pt bold in navy; subtitle 18–20 pt bold in dark grey; body 10–12 pt; table text 9–10 pt; footnotes 8–9 pt grey.
- **Colours:**
  - Navy `#192890`: titles, table header rows, primary card headers
  - Royal blue `#2D3EAA`: secondary headers
  - Teal `#28B2AE`: accent: labels, "start here" highlights, callout borders, flow-step outlines
  - Light teal `#E3F5F4`: callout bars and highlighted quadrant
  - Lavender `#ECEFFA`: alternating table rows and card bodies
  - Text: `#1F1F1F` (body) and `#595959` (secondary)
  - Background: white
- **Title slide and section dividers:** full navy background, a large pale-blue section number ("01"), and the white section title.
- **Content slides:** white, with a thin grey rule at the top left, the title in navy, and a one-line grey subtitle under it explaining what the slide shows. Slide number bottom right.
- **Tables:** navy header row with white bold text, lavender/white banded rows, thin grey borders, no heavy shading.
- **Cards:** rounded rectangles with a navy (or teal) header band holding the label in white caps (e.g. "PERSONA 1"), the name in bold below it, and the body on lavender or white.
- **Callout bar:** a full-width light-teal strip at the bottom of a slide with a bold lead-in ("What sets us apart: …").
- **Density:** these are dense working slides, but keep at least 0.4" margins, nothing below 8 pt, and no overflow. If text doesn't fit, shrink the font one step before cutting content. Never cut quotes or codes.

### Slide 13: Divider "06 · MVP"

### Slide 14: What is your MVP? Why are things in the MVP? What is minimum about it?
**Top:** a 5-step flow of outlined rounded boxes joined by grey arrows. Step 4 is filled light teal.
`1 Add interview (JD)` → `2 Find and load stories` → `3 Mock answer` → `4 Pinned feedback` → `5 Retry, compare`

**Middle:** four module cards in a row. Cards 1–2 have navy headers, card 3 teal, card 4 royal blue labelled "(light)". Each card has: an italic one-line user promise, bold item IDs, a grey "P1" line, a "**Why**" line and a "**Measured by**" line.

1. **Mock and feedback**: *"Show me where my answer lost the listener, then let me fix it"*
   - MF1 3–5 questions from the JD's competencies
   - MF2 Voice answer, timestamped transcript
   - MF3 One follow-up per answer that probes a vague claim
   - MF4 Delivery feedback pinned to his lines: time to decision, I vs we, length
   - MF5 Fit feedback: answered the question? competency proved? No rewrite
   - MF6 Retry and see before vs after
   - MF7 Mark any point "not useful"
   - P1: strict or supportive mode · body-language fake door
   - **Why:** feedback is generic today (15 of 25) · **Measured by:** ≥60% retry after feedback
2. **Story builder**: *"Put my decision first, without inflating it, and match it to the role"*
   - SB1 Paste stories or a doc into a story bank
   - SB2 Decision first: one-line situation, his decision, result, in his words
   - SB3 Split team vs his actions; flag "we" and claims without evidence
   - SB4 4–6 JD competencies quoted from the JD, mapped to stories, gaps shown
   - SB5 Never adds ownership, numbers or outcomes
   - P1: company-specific tailoring
   - **Why:** rewrites overstated roles (SUR-1, SUR-2, SUR-3) · **Measured by:** ≥60% of stories marked ready
3. **Story discovery (gaps)**: *"When a JD ask has no story, help me find one"*
   - SD1 For each gap, 3–5 prompts drawn from his CV and past roles
   - SD2 Turns his answer into a draft story in the bank, nothing invented
   - P1: full guided brainstorm from scratch
   - **Why:** can't see his own strongest work (ROH-2, ROH-1) · **Measured by:** ≥1 gap filled per user
4. **Prep list (light)**: *"Tell me what to rehearse before this interview"*
   - PL1 From the date and JD: 3–5 stories and questions to rehearse, in 20-min blocks
   - PL2 List updates as stories are marked ready
   - P1: daily nudges at a chosen time, missed-day reshuffle
   - **Why:** story prep is squeezed behind cases (SUR-1, SUR-3, ADD-2) · **Measured by:** ≥40% return in week 2 unprompted

**Bottom (two light-teal bars):**
- "**What is minimum about it:** fit and behavioural only · one interview at a time · one fix per retry · no sign-up, no nudges · a teammate checks the feedback for the first 5 users"
- "**The test it must pass:** 15 ISB candidates, 2 weeks · ≥60% retry after feedback · ≥50% of retries reach the decision 30% faster · ≥40% return unprompted · <25% of feedback marked not useful"

### Slide 15: Goals and Non-Goals for your MVP
