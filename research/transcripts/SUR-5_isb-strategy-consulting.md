### **Interview 5: SUR-5 (strategy and consulting)**

I5-1 **[Suraj]** Thanks, SUR-5. I'm looking at how classmates prepare for placements. We have about six minutes. Is it okay to record this for the assignment, shared only with the course team?

I5-2 **[SUR-5]** Yeah, fine. I haven't done as many mocks as some people, though. That might be useful or completely useless for your project.

I5-3 **[Suraj]** What roles are you preparing for?

I5-4 **[SUR-5]** Strategy and consulting... mostly. I worked in a boutique consulting firm in Mumbai for four years before ISB. I've been through client presentations and a few hiring processes. That doesn't mean I'm sorted, but explaining my own projects isn't my biggest concern right now. Cases with unfamiliar industries are taking more effort. **J**

I5-5 **[Suraj]** Tell me about your most recent practice session.

I5-6 **[SUR-5]** Monday afternoon. Uh, with a classmate. We did a market entry case and then ten minutes of fit. He asked about a difficult client conversation. I used a story from an implementation project. He asked two follow-ups about why we escalated and what I would change. I answered those, we moved on. His main feedback was that I'd missed a distribution cost in the case. **J G**

I5-7 **[Suraj]** How had you prepared that personal example?

I5-8 **[SUR-5]** I hadn't written a full answer, no. I have a small sheet with six incidents, the decision I made, and one result for each. I looked at it for... five minutes before the mock. I've told that client story before, including

to people outside work. So I know which details need explaining. The exact sentences change each time. That actually helps me listen to the question. **J G**

#### I5-9 **[Suraj]** Has that ever... gone wrong?

I5-10 **[SUR-5]** Yeah. In a mock a few weeks ago, I gave the example I had ready instead of the one the person asked for. The question was about failing, and I gave something where we eventually succeeded. It was a nice story. Wrong question. My partner called that out. I changed the example, not the wording. I don't think another polished version of the same story would have helped. **P**

I5-11 **[Suraj]** What did you do after that session?

I5-12 **[SUR-5]** Added... a genuinely unsuccessful project to the sheet. I wrote what I missed and what happened because of it. Took about fifteen minutes. Something like that. Then I tried it with my study partner the next day. He asked what I had done differently afterwards. I had an example for that as well, so we were done. I didn't make five versions or record it. **J G**

I5-13 **[Suraj]** What makes that feedback trustworthy for you?

I5-14 **[SUR-5]** He asks difficult questions, and he doesn't agree with everything I say. Also he points to... something I can recognise. You answered a different question. That's specific. If he tells me to use a word I wouldn't normally use, I ignore it. Sometimes we disagree and that's okay. We both know one mock interviewer isn't a hiring committee. **G Q**

I5-15 **[Suraj]** Mm. What tools have you tried for preparation?

I5-16 **[SUR-5]** Alumni casebooks, a spreadsheet for case errors, and notes on my phone. I tried an AI mock for about twenty minutes one evening. It was convenient. Yeah. The follow-ups were too easy in that session, though. I said I managed a difficult stakeholder and it more or less accepted that. My friend would ask what the stakeholder actually said and whether I was the difficult one. **J P**

#### I5-17 **[Suraj]** Did you use it again?

I5-18 **[SUR-5]** Once. For a list of industry questions. That was useful. I haven't gone back for personal interview feedback. It's not a... strong habit for me. If my partner cancels, I usually do a case calculation or read an industry note instead. There's enough other preparation to fill the time. I don't feel blocked because I can't get feedback on my story that evening. **J G**

I5-19 **[Suraj]** How much preparation time went into this past week?

I5-20 **[SUR-5]** Um... around five hours outside the peer mocks, I think. Mostly cases and industry reading. Personal answers... maybe half an hour total. I've had two peer sessions. I haven't paid for coaching. Someone suggested a package, but I didn't investigate because our group is working for me. I'm not saying paid coaching is bad. I just haven't had a reason to add it. **J**

I5-21 **[Suraj]** How do you tell whether the current approach is working?

I5-22 **[SUR-5]** For cases, I track repeated errors. Am I still missing the same cost, or rushing into a calculation? For personal answers, it's... less formal. Can I answer the actual question and handle the follow-up without changing the facts? The last two partners didn't need me to explain my role again. That's encouraging, but a real interview can still go differently. **J G**

I5-23 **[Suraj]** Is there any part that still feels awkward?

I5-24 **[SUR-5]** Choosing how much detail for somebody who doesn't know consulting. I sometimes use client language without noticing. A friend from healthcare caught that last month. So... I do benefit from a fresh listener. I just don't need that listener every day. Once the confusion is fixed, doing another ten attempts can make me sound rehearsed, at least to myself. **P G Q**

I5-25 **[Suraj]** Anything else I should have asked?

I5-26 **[SUR-5]** Maybe... ask people what's already working before looking for gaps. My group is reliable and fairly blunt. If that disappeared, my preparation would change. Right now, adding another score or dashboard would probably become one more thing I don't open. Anyway... happy to introduce you to someone who prepares very differently. **G Q**

I5-27 **[Suraj]** What keeps your peer sessions reliable?

I5-28 **[SUR-5]** We agree the slot before the week starts. Nothing fancy. One case each, then whichever personal question somebody wants to try. If one of us hasn't prepared a case, we say so before the call. Saves that awkward ten minutes of searching while the other person waits. We do cancel sometimes. Last week we moved a session because of a submission. I did some calculations on my own that evening. It wasn't a crisis. That's partly why I haven't looked very hard for another arrangement. **J G**

I5-29 **[Suraj]** When did a fresh listener last notice something your regular partner missed?

I5-30 **[SUR-5]** The healthcare friend I mentioned. I used a client term and she asked what it meant. My partner knew it, so he'd never stopped me there. I replaced it with a plain description and tried the sentence again. That took... maybe a minute. I didn't rewrite the story. It reminded me that familiar people can fill in gaps without noticing. So occasionally I ask somebody outside the group. Not another full mock necessarily. Sometimes it's just, can I tell you this example, and where did I lose you? **J G**

I5-31 **[Suraj]** Great. Thanks, SUR-5, I'll leave it there.

I5-32 **[SUR-5]** Sure. Hope that was useful.
