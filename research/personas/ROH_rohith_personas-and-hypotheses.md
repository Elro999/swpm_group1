Problem hypotheses are framed using the template: _The [type] of [Customer] are most frustrated about [Job-To-Be-Done] because [Problem]._

### **<u>Problem Hypothesis 1:</u>**

The **master's students and new grads job-hunting off-campus** are most frustrated about **getting realistic practice and feedback before the real interview** because they have no placement ecosystem, their peers don't know the target field and interviewers don't give feedback, so they learn by failing real interviews and burn shortlists they can't get back.

### **<u>Problem Hypothesis 2:</u>**

The **frequent interviewers** are most frustrated about **learning from each interview they give** because the lessons fade within a day or two, and the only ways to keep them today are notes written that night or recording the interview against policy.

### **Additional Problem Hypotheses**

### **<u>Problem Hypothesis 3:</u>**

The **professionals who interview occasionally** are most frustrated about **turning their experience into a confident, role-specific story** because they can't judge which achievements matter to an outsider, and feedback from friends and family is either too scattered or not about the target role.

### **<u>Problem Hypothesis 4:</u>**

The **off-campus job seekers** are most frustrated about **predicting what a specific interviewer will ask** because that knowledge sits with alumni and insiders, and collecting it takes 2–3 days of networking for every interview.

_Scope note:_ mid-career job switchers likely face the same lack of support as the Solo JobHunter, but none of the five interviewees belong to that group, so they are not covered here.
